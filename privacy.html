Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

🔒 Your data stays on your device. iGymBro stores all workout data locally using SwiftData. We do not operate any servers, do not collect personal information, and do not share your data with third parties.

1. Overview

iGymBro ("we", "our", or "the app") is a fitness tracking application developed for iPhone and Apple Watch. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and your rights with respect to that information.

By using iGymBro, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Access

2.1 HealthKit Data

iGymBro requests access to Apple HealthKit to enhance your workout experience. With your explicit permission, the app reads:

  • Heart Rate — displayed in real time during active workout sessions
  • Active Energy Burned (Calories) — displayed in real time during active workout sessions

HealthKit data is:

  • Only accessed during active workout sessions
  • Never uploaded to any server or shared with any third party
  • Processed on-device only, in real time
  • Not stored in iGymBro's own database — only displayed temporarily during the session

You can revoke HealthKit access at any time via iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → iGymBro.

2.2 Workout and Training Data

The app stores the following data locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework:

  • Workout templates and programs you create
  • Exercise definitions (including any custom exercises you add)
  • Completed workout sessions with sets, reps, and weights
  • Weekly training schedules
  • Personal records (PRs) with dates
  • App preferences (such as language selection)

This data is stored exclusively in the app's private container on your device. It is included in your iPhone's encrypted local backup (iCloud Backup or iTunes Backup) if you have backups enabled.

2.3 Apple Watch Sync

When you use the iGymBro Apple Watch app, workout data is synced between your iPhone and Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. This communication occurs directly between your devices over your personal Bluetooth or Wi-Fi connection. No data leaves your devices during this process.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

iGymBro does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or any other personal identifiers
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Contacts or calendar data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports to any third-party service
  • Any data to any server operated by us or third parties

4. No Third-Party Services

iGymBro does not integrate any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, tracking libraries, or data-sharing frameworks. The only external frameworks used are Apple's own (SwiftData, HealthKit, WatchConnectivity), which are subject to Apple's privacy policies.

5. Data Storage and Security

All data created within iGymBro is stored in your iPhone's local app container, protected by iOS's built-in encryption and sandboxing. The app does not have its own account system or cloud storage.

Your data is as secure as your device — we strongly encourage you to use a device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID.

6. Data Deletion

To delete all data stored by iGymBro, simply delete the app from your iPhone. This permanently removes all locally stored workout data, templates, and preferences. Data previously included in iCloud Backup may persist in your backups until those backups expire or are deleted according to Apple's backup policies.

7. Children's Privacy

iGymBro is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has used this app and you have concerns, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
